Spotlight · HP (I & E) / Fortinet B2B order loading

Five documents in, one validated IFS order out

5 documentsread and cross-checked per order
Up to 6 IFS recordscreated automatically per order
~1 hr saved†per order; nothing is loaded silently

How an order flows

  • Upload — sales upload the packet on the web portal: partner PO, end-customer PO, OEM approval, credit approval and pricing sheet (Fortinet: quote, PO, credit approval, licence sheet).
  • Read — OCR and AI pull customer, parts, quantities, prices, approval references and validity dates from every document, whatever the layout.
  • Check — documents are reconciled with each other and IFS master data — prices, quantities, approvals, credit, parts and customers — each rule scored PASS / WARNING / ERROR.
  • Approve — failed or doubtful checks go to a manager; approved exceptions continue, rejected ones stop.
  • Load — campaign, master part, inventory part, supplier link, customer order and purchase order are created in IFS; sales get a verification dashboard by email.

Business impact

  • Speed — an order that needed a specialist to open five files and key several IFS screens is booked in minutes once approved.
  • Accuracy — pricing, quantity and approval mismatches are caught before anything reaches the OEM or the customer.
  • Control — every order carries its documents, its checks and its approver; a complete audit trail by design.
  • Scalability — one flow already serves HP Inc, HPE and Fortinet; other OEMs join without new manual processes.
  • Capacity — ~1 hour of expert time returned per order†; in use by Mumbai, Bangalore, Delhi, Gurgaon, Jaipur, Ahmedabad and Bhopal.
